# Opening quality playtest

Use a fresh slot to see the new finite animal habitat. Existing saves retain their surviving animals and provisions. The service log now opens with current work, stove/rack stock, prepared water and walkie status. Public reports remain optional live broadcasts.

## First session — let the player discover the loop

Give an unfamiliar player the controls, without narrating the objectives. Record the time at which they understand their assignment, discover the service log, take the walkie, inspect the ground cabinet, recognize the logging track, reach the reserved depot relay and return. Ask what they think changed in the world and what they would do tomorrow. Do not coach them into the intended answer.

Before the first night, observe whether they notice finite wood, the remaining stove burn, parallel water/food preparation and the sleeping berth. Success means making a return plan from resources and daylight, not completing every optional work stop. If they need the reference manual open constantly, the opening still needs clearer environmental teaching.

## Separate acceptance runs

| Run | Deliberate condition | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Silent news | Receiver off, walkie left behind or exhausted | Cabinet, depot, store and clinic remain understandable through local evidence; missed reports are not replayed. |
| Exhausted support | Home battery zero before replacing and verifying the relay | Commissioning reconnects solar charging; recovery threshold and night/cloud/snow obstruction are legible at the desk. |
| Optional jobs ignored | Follow only the assignment | Return and first-night preparation remain viable; side tasks improve readiness without becoming compulsory. |
| Hunting | Find naturally generated deer off the logging route | Cover and crouching permit an approach; loud movement/shots still alert prey. Killed animals do not reappear after reload. |
| Food | Harvest with pack near capacity, cook, dry and sleep | Overflow remains recoverable; stock, heat, age and energy stay consistent. Raw portions explain that they require cooking. |
| Storm and injury | Travel with a wound and worsening weather | Surface/sky contrast keeps the route readable; treatment, water, warmth and fatigue matter without obscuring interaction prompts. |
| Changed town | Visit the clinic before and after the collapse | Two exits, hostile approach and finite treatment stock support entry, avoidance and retreat; the player can describe the change without hearing every broadcast. |
| Reload | Save partway through food preparation, after a kill and after assignment completion | Timers, remaining animals, carcass age, power state and personal story evidence persist; stock does not refill. |

## Visual and motion review

Compare forest foliage, supported snow volumes and bark at walking distance, across LOD changes, and at dusk. Check that the new trees do not feel thinner or more repetitive despite the measured geometry and unchanged tree population. Review the compound doorway in a blizzard and the return route at night. Reject any loss of navigational readability.

Watch empty hands, bare/gloved drinking, treatment, service-tool work, firearm aiming and reloads at ordinary player speed. Judge fingers against the held object rather than accepting a rig merely because its transforms are finite. The flask mouth should be open during a sip, the cap should remain on stored/pickup bottles, and sleeves should remain attached.

Watch infected approach, wind-up, contact, recovery, obstruction, stagger, crawling and pool reuse. Bone/vertex checks protect against distortion; they do not establish convincing acting, foot planting or death performance.

## Radio performance production brief

The current local speech is development performance. Final delivery needs distinct restrained public/news and dispatch voices, clean edited takes, station identity, authentic pauses and escalating uncertainty. Keep the existing bulletin timings, durations, single-delivery rules and dispatch exclusion windows. The first day should sound like ordinary weather/work before reports become contradictory; later silence should be meaningful rather than filled with invented threats. Export dry takes separately from the existing radio filtering, match perceived loudness, and verify intelligibility both at the desk and on the carried walkie during wind and footsteps.

## Decision record

For each run record seed/save, duration, route, weather, resources on departure/return, failures, interpretation of the story, and whether the visuals are an improvement. Mark human acceptance only after someone plays these runs; automated checks and staged screenshots are separate evidence.
